# Media Buying Advisor

Decision support for paid-media managers in financial lead generation: diagnose an account with unit economics, read the auction, compare ad copy, and rank concrete actions by estimated profit uplift. Model version **0.2.0**.

## The problem
Advice on search and social buying is usually a list of tactics without a way to compare them. This tool organises it around one identity, `ROAS = CVR x EPL / CPC` (break-even CPC = CVR x EPL), and four levers (CPC, click-to-lead conversion, EPL, spend volume).

## Worked example (ROAS 90%)
Spend 20,000 EUR/month, CPC 0.95 EUR, click-to-lead 9%, impression share 15%: EPL 9.50 EUR, break-even CPC 0.855 EUR, profit -2,000 EUR/month.
A five-action plan (`cpl, excl, lowq, neg, lp`) lifts ROAS to **115% / 142% / 173%** (low / mid / high) after a 3-month ramp.

Top actions by mid-case monthly profit impact: `lp` 3,649 EUR; `track` 3,060 EUR; `ads` 2,916 EUR; `neg` 2,754 EUR; `lowq` 2,381 EUR.

## Assumptions
- Effect ranges of the 15 actions are **hypotheses from practitioner knowledge, not measured elasticities**; they live in `data/params.json` and should be replaced by test results.
- Combined actions are multiplied per lever with a damping factor of 0.7; the CPC improves towards its most negative end.
- Capacity: CPC rises 30.0% for every 1% of extra clicks when raising impression share.
- Below break-even (ROAS < 100%) volume levers lose money; the tool shows this instead of hiding it.

## Data and what is validated
Nine anonymised accounts in five markets are the reference portfolio (monthly spend and ROAS, auction insights, competitor mentions, campaign snapshot); see [`data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`](data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md). There is no ground truth for action uplift, so the **backtest is not available for this product**: what the tests do verify is the accounting (ROAS identity, break-even, profit = revenue - spend), the worked example above, ordering of ranges for non-volume actions, and that the HTML prototype gives the same numbers as the Python model. Account-level reconciliation: `revenue / spend` of the model equals the reported ROAS of every account-month.

## Known limits
- One account at a time; no cross-campaign cannibalisation.
- Auction-insight shares are platform-reported and partial; competitor labels are anonymised.
- Ad-copy analysis is regex-based claim coverage in Spanish; it does not read competitor ads from public libraries.
- Impact ranges are hypotheses; prioritise by running controlled tests.
